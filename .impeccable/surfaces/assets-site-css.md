# Course site (all pages)

Scope: every page of the private course site (home, weeks index, week page, resources, student page, message pages). Mode: Read.
Audience and job: a student (grades 6 to 12) on a laptop finds this week's question, what to have ready, and the tasks, then submits. Parents check dates and notes.
Constraints: content comes from Markdown; pages will gain diagrams, videos, code and fill-in tables; minimal, smooth, few animations, simple for students.
Memorable moment: the highlighter stroke that sweeps across the one thing to do now.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Swarit's own notebook, opened at this week. It refuses the course-portal default of white cards, progress bars and tiles.

OWN-WORLD: One continuous unlined sheet of slightly yellowed, toothy paper with visible fibre grain and a soft vignette. Graphite-black ink for text, pencil grey for margin notes and rules, fountain-pen blue for links, fluorescent yellow highlighter laid with multiply. Figures, videos and code are taped in with translucent paper tape. A reading serif for the column, a handwritten face only for margin notes and annotations, mono only for code.

STORY: A student sees this week's question in big ink, the highlighted line telling them what to do now, and the next class date in the margin. They read down the page like notes, do the tasks, and submit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: running head (wordmark left, page date right). Left margin carries pencil notes (cohort, next class). Column: "Hi Aarav" in large serif, this week's question underneath, one highlighted line "Open week 2 before Sunday" with an ink-ringed button. Below the fold, the contents page: 12 weeks with dotted leaders to dates.

FORM: Mentor's notebook, #1 on my ordered list (chosen by the user as my pick over the assigned drawing-sheet direction). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
