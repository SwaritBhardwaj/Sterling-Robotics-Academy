# Mentor claims

Use this exact wording in every public piece.

## Allowed
- "Taught by Swarit Bhardwaj, former robotics researcher at IIT Bombay."
- Industry: autonomous vehicles, Swaayatt Robots.
- Gold medalist, IndiaSkills Mechatronics (never add "State").
- Gold medalist, Mechatronics Engineering (university).
- Postgraduate studies, LaunchPilot Singapore.
- 100+ students taught, 1,000+ hours of teaching, 3 years, "the US and India" (in that order).
- Logos: IIT Bombay, Swaayatt Robots, the full LaunchPilot wordmark.

## Not allowed
- MBA, "business school", master's.
- "LaunchPilot Singapore" as text in the logo row.
- "India and the US" (wrong order).
- Mentor universities. Mentors from TUM, CMU and IITs are planned but unconfirmed. Never name them.
- Promised class times.
- Invented testimonials or photos.
