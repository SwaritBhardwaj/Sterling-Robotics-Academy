# Platform choice

Why we built our own course site instead of using Thinkific, Skool, Google Classroom and others, and why Cloudflare: `02-PROJECTS/course-site/decisions.md`.

Short version: for 2 to 10 students, platforms cost $50 to $100 a month or take 10%, and many don't suit under-13s. Cloudflare is the only free host with a login gate.
