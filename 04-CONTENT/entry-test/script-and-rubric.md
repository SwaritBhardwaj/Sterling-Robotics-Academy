# Entry test: call script and rubric

_A free, live 10-minute video call. No written test. Tool and setup: `02-PROJECTS/entry-test/overview.md`. Questions: `question-bank.md`._

## Why
Admission and placement that a child can't hand to AI. It checks what they have built, how they reason, physics and circuits. Experience alone does not decide it.

## Timing
| Start | Part | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 1. What they have built | 2.5 min |
| 2:30 | 2. Thinking puzzles | 2 min |
| 4:30 | 3. Wheels and motion | 2.5 min |
| 7:00 | 4. Circuits on paper | 2.5 min |
| 9:30 | Wrap-up | 0.5 min |

## Parts (each scored 0 to 3)

**1. What they have built.** "Tell me about something you built or made yourself. Anything counts: Lego, a school project, a game, a robot." "Show it on camera." "What went wrong and how did you fix it?" "Have you written code? What did you make?" If Arduino: "What did you connect, and to which pin?"
- 0 nothing · 1 kits, Lego or block code · 2 own build or text code, and names a real fix · 3 a working electronics or robot project explained in their own words

**2. Thinking puzzles.** A robot path puzzle ("You are the robot...") and a number pattern, then "How did you get that?"
- 0 neither, even with a hint · 1 one, with a hint · 2 both, or one unaided with clear reasoning · 3 both quickly with clear steps

**3. Wheels and motion.** Gear turns and force, wheel distance per turn, a bonus.
- 0 nothing sensible · 1 some right but guessing · 2 first two right with reasons · 3 bonus too, with a reason

**4. Circuits on paper.** Draw a circuit and hold it to the camera, a circuit question, a bonus.
- 0 no closed loop even with help · 1 a working loop with help · 2 correct drawing plus the next question · 3 bonus too, with why

## Integrity ticks
**Signs of outside help:** eyes on another screen · long silence then a textbook answer · can't explain their own answer · whispering or typing off camera · words they can't explain.
**Good signs** (for borderline calls) are ticked too.

## Result bands (out of 12)
| Score | Result |
|---|---|
| 7 or more | Admit |
| 4 to 6 | Admit with prep (Wokwi "Blink" task, about 30 min) |
| 3 or less | Not ready yet |
| 2+ outside-help ticks | Retest |

Result messages (Admit, Admit with prep, Not ready yet) are generated by the grader with names, dates and the payment link filled in.

## Before the call (message to the parent, also in grader Settings)
```
Hi [Parent], thanks for booking the entry test for [Child]. It's a 10-minute video call with me, and no preparation is needed.

Please have ready:
• A laptop or phone with a camera
• A pen and a few sheets of blank paper
• Anything [Child] has built (optional)

[Child] should sit at a desk alone with the camera on. You're welcome to stay nearby.
```

## Reminder on the day
```
Hi [Parent], a quick reminder that [Child]'s entry test is today at [time, time zone].

Here's the video call link: [Meet link]

Please have a pen and paper ready, with the camera on. See you soon.
```
