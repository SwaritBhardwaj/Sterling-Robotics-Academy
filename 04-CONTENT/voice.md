# Voice

## Tone
Gentle, polite, welcoming (Swarit's words for WhatsApp). Calm and trustworthy for parents, inviting for a 12-year-old.

## Rules
- Plain, short sentences. Lead with the point.
- No em dashes. No AI-sounding words (delve, leverage, robust). No "not X, it's Y" lines.
- Concrete facts and numbers over adjectives.
- Never say "reasoning test". The test is "a 10-minute call" or "the free entry test".
- Say "about $50 a week" next to "$600 total, robot kit included".
- "You pay nothing until your child is admitted."
- "Limited seats." Never a number.
- Class time: "Every Sunday, at a time confirmed before class one."

## For students (site)
Second person, short. One thing to do now. Questions before answers.

## For parents
What the child will build, what it costs, what they need to do next. One link.
