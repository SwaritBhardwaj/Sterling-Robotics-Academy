Questions
Questions

The ones parents ask first.

Does my child need experience?

Not required. Some coding or Arduino experience helps.

What is the entry test?

Free. A 10‑minute video call with Swarit. It checks thinking, not prior robotics.

How much time each week?

Up to 90 minutes live, 1 to 4 hours of tasks, and a 30‑minute one‑on‑one.

What time are the classes?

Every Sunday, at a time confirmed before class one. Every class is recorded.

What if we change our mind?

Full refund before your cohort’s deadline, $75 back until kits ship, no refund after.

What if my child gets stuck?

They bring it to their weekly one‑on‑one, or ask the class group any day.

Do we need to buy anything?

Only a laptop. The robot kit is included.

Will this help with college?

It gives your child a real engineering project to show in applications.
