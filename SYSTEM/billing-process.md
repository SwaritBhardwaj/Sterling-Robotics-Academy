# Billing process

_How money moves. Escalate every refund and every exception to Swarit._

## Flow per family
1. Free entry test. Nothing is paid before admission.
2. Admitted → $150 reserve link sent with the result message (counts toward $600).
3. $450 balance due a week before class one (founding: 8 Nov 2026; winter: 3 Jan 2027).
4. Balance paid → shipping address confirmed → kit ships.
5. Referral credit: $50 off for the new family at payment; $50 to the referrer once the child is admitted.

## Kit funding (decided 2026-10-15)

- The refund policy stays as published. By the time kits are bought, the full-refund window has closed, so there is no clash.
