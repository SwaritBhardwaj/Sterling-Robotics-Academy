# Billing process

_How money moves. Escalate every refund and every exception to Swarit._

## Flow per family
1. Free entry test. Nothing is paid before admission.
2. Admitted → $150 reserve link sent with the result message (counts toward $600).
3. $450 balance due a week before class one (founding: 8 Nov 2026; winter: 3 Jan 2027).
4. Balance paid → shipping address confirmed → kit ships.
5. Referral credit: $50 off for the new family at payment; $50 to the referrer once the child is admitted.

## Payment portal
- **Infinity app.** Payment link not set yet. KYC for an international bank account in progress (Oct 2026).
- Before the first real payment: run a $1 test end to end.
- To decide: how refunds are paid back, and how referral credits are paid out.

## Refunds
| When | Refund |
|---|---|
| Before the enrolment deadline | Full |
| After the deadline, before kits ship | $75 back |

## Tax and banking (India, export of services)

- Keep a **FIRA** for each foreign payment.
