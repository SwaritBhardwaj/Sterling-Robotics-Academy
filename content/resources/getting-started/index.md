## How this site works
- A new week opens on the Monday before each Sunday class.
- Each week's page has the question we explore, what to have ready, the assignment and the files you need.
- The class recording is added to the week's page after class.
- **My page** has notes from your 1:1 sessions and any extra tasks just for you.

## Every week
1. Read the week's page before Sunday and get the things under **Have ready for class**.
2. Join the live class. The link is on the home page.
3. Do the assignment. Plan for 1 to 4 hours across the week.
4. Submit your work with the **Submit** button on the week's page.
5. Book your 30-minute 1:1 with Swarit or a mentor.

## Stuck?
- Re-watch the part of the recording you missed.
- Try the four-step debugging card from week 4.
- Ask on the class WhatsApp group, or bring it to your 1:1.
