---
title: Free tools we use
---

- **phyphox** ([phyphox.org](https://phyphox.org)): turns your phone into a sensor lab. Free on iOS and Android. Used from week 1.
- **Wokwi** ([wokwi.com](https://wokwi.com)): runs Arduino code in your browser with no hardware. Used in week 1 and for practice.
- **Tinkercad Circuits** ([tinkercad.com](https://www.tinkercad.com)): build and test circuits in the browser. Make a free account before week 3.
- **Arduino IDE 2** ([arduino.cc/en/software](https://www.arduino.cc/en/software)): where you write and upload code to the robot. See [Set up Arduino IDE 2](../arduino-setup/).
