> How do you keep something standing that wants to fall?

This week you feel the problem with your own hands, find out that your phone can feel tilt, and run your first code. Read the four short pages first. Each ends with a quick quiz that earns Volts. Then do the tasks in order.

## Have ready for class
- A broom or mop, a 30 cm ruler and a pencil
- phyphox installed on a phone (free on iOS and Android)
- Laptop with Chrome open
- Camera on, phone nearby
