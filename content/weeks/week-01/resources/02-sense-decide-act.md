Every machine that balances itself does three things, over and over.

![The loop runs again and again, faster than the fall.](img/sense-decide-act.svg)

## Sense
Notice what is happening. When you balance a broom, your **eyes** see it start to lean.

## Decide
Work out what to do about it. Your **brain** decides: "It leans left, move my hand left."

## Act
Do it. Your **hand** moves.

Then the loop starts again. If you close your eyes, the loop breaks at the first step and the broom falls. If the loop is slower than the fall, the broom falls too.

## Try it
Next time you balance the broom, say "sense, decide, act" in your head. Notice how many times you go round the loop in ten seconds.

In week 2 you will find the robot's eyes, brain and hands. For now, guess which parts they might be.

## Quick quiz
1. When you balance a broom, which part of you does the "sense" step?
   - Your hand
   - Your eyes
   - Your feet
2. Why does the broom fall when you close your eyes?
   - The broom gets heavier
   - The loop has no "sense" step, so you can't correct
   - Your hand gets tired
3. What happens if the loop runs slower than the fall?
   - The object falls before you can correct it
   - Nothing changes
   - It balances better
