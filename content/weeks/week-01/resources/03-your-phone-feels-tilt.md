Your phone has a tiny sensor that feels which way is down. The robot has the same kind of chip. Let's see it working.


_Swarit's short video goes here. Until then, follow the steps below._

## Try it on your phone
1. Install **phyphox** (free on iOS and Android).
2. Open **Acceleration with g** and press the play button.
3. Put the phone flat on the table, screen up. Look for the number near **9.8**.
4. Slowly stand the phone on its long edge. Watch the 9.8 move to a different line.
5. Flip the phone over. What happens to the sign?

The phone isn't moving, yet one number says 9.8. Hold on to that puzzle. It opens week 5.
