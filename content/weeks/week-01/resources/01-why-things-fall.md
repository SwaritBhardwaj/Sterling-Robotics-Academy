Stand a broom on your open palm. Now try a pencil. The broom is easy. The pencil falls almost at once. Why would the bigger, heavier thing be easier?

## Short things fall faster
Everything that is tipping over falls a little at first, then faster and faster. A tall object takes longer to get going. A pencil tips over in a blink, so your hand has no time to catch it. A broom gives you about a second to react. ==A longer object buys you time.==

## Balancing is not standing still
Watch your hand while you balance the broom. It never stops moving. Each time the broom leans a little, your hand slides under it. Balancing means making small corrections, all the time, faster than the fall.

## Your robot has the same problem
The Tumbller is short, like the pencil. It starts to fall in a fraction of a second. So it has to correct itself hundreds of times every second. By week 10, your code will do that.
