When your kit arrives: unbox it, check every part against the parts list, and take one photo of the parts laid out. Charge the battery the night before class. Install Arduino IDE 2.

Paste a link to the photo (Google Drive or Google Photos, shared so anyone with the link can view) and write "battery charged" and "IDE installed" when they are done.
