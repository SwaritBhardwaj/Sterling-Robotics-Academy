How to run the test
  • Camera on. Face and both hands in view. No other tabs or phones on the desk.
  • Share only the slides window in Meet. Keep this page on your phone.
  • Read each question aloud while it's on the slide. After every answer, ask "Why?" or "How did you get that?"
  • Give one hint at most per question, and count it in the score.
  • A parent can sit nearby but doesn't answer.
  • For a retest, press New test on the slides. This page picks up the new questions.

Signs of outside help

Two or more ticks: press New test on the slides and redo parts 2 to 4.

Good signs

Not scored. Useful when a score sits on the line.
