# Curriculum overview

## Goal
By week 11, every robot stands on its own for 30 seconds and recovers from three taps.
Not in this cohort: wheel encoders, speed control, sensor fusion with the ultrasonic. Ultrasonic stop and Bluetooth control are optional extras in week 12.

## Approach
Inquiry-based. Each week opens with a question about a real physical problem, explored by hand before theory or code. Five phases per class: Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate. Full rules: `teaching-guide/00-overview.md`.

## The 12 weeks

| # | Site title, driving question | Kit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why is balancing hard? How do you keep something standing that wants to fall? | No kit (broom, phyphox, Wokwi) |
| 2 | Build the robot. What does each part do in the sense, decide, act loop? | Kit |
| 3 | Statements, variables and conditions. How does a robot decide what to do? | Laptop only |
| 4 | Loops, the Serial Monitor and debugging. How do we see what the robot is thinking? | Kit |
| 5 | Inside the motion sensor. How does a tiny chip feel tilt and turning? | Kit |
| 6 | A clean tilt angle, and how distance is measured. One sensor jitters, the other drifts. | Kit |
| 7 | Actuators: PWM and the motor driver. How does a pin make a motor go slowly? | Kit |
| 8 | Control: P and D. How hard should the wheels push, and when? | Kit |
| 9 | Balance point, safety cut-off and I. Why does my robot run off one way? | Kit |
| 10 | Push recovery: tuning and debugging. How do we make it stand back up? | Kit |
| 11 | Submission week. Can I explain every line of my robot's code? | Kit |
| 12 | Iterate, final video and demo day. What can you show someone new? | Kit |

Parent-facing names (brochure): Why things fall · Build the robot · First real code · Make it talk back · The robot's inner ear · Which way is up · Wheels that obey · How drones stay steady · Standing tall · Bounce back · Finish line · Demo day.

Coding weeks come before kit-heavy weeks because kits may arrive late. Control is P+D in week 8; balance point, cut-off, dead zone and I in week 9; push recovery in week 10.

## Weekly format
- One live class every Sunday, 60 to 90 minutes (the guide plans 85 to 90, with a short version for 50 to 60). Recorded.
- A 30-minute 1:1 with Swarit or a mentor he trains.
- 1 to 4 hours of tasks. Due Saturday 8 PM ET.
- A class WhatsApp group with parents.

## Schedule rules (site)
- A week opens 6 days before its class (the Monday before). Founding week 1 opens 9 Nov.
- Solutions open 6 days after class (the Saturday after).
- No breaks for November or Thanksgiving in the founding cohort.

## Student week page format (site repo)
- Header: `title`, `short`, `kit`, `recording` (unlisted YouTube link after class), `slides`.
- First line after the header: `> the driving question`.
- Sections: "By the end of this week", "Have ready for class", "Assignment" (`### Must: ...`, `### Stretch: ...`, `### Optional: ...`, `### Bonus: ...`), "Before you submit, check", "Resources".
- Fill-in templates go in code blocks (they get a copy button).

## Tools students use
phyphox (weeks 1, 6) · Wokwi (week 1) · Tinkercad Circuits (weeks 3, 7) · Arduino IDE 2 (weeks 2 to 12) · Elegoo Tumbller manual and code pack (weeks 2, 5, 7). All free.
