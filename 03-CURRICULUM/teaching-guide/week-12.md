# Week 12: Iterate, final video and demo day

> Teacher-facing. Converted from `sterling-teaching-guide.html` (the HTML teaching guide) on 2026-10-15. Student version of this week lives in the site repo: `content/weeks/week-12/index.md`.

**Driving question:** What can you show, and explain, to someone who has never seen this before?

| | |
|---|---|
| Class length | 90 min, 75 min short |
| Robot by the end of the week | Final video and demo day |
| Assignment due | Saturday, 8 PM ET |

## Goal

Students polish their robot, record a final video, and present to parents on demo day. Optional extensions (ultrasonic stop, Bluetooth control) are offered as future projects.

Students leave with:
- A final video they can share
- A certificate
- A next project

## Before class

You prepare:
- A 5-minute montage edited from every final video
- 8 to 10 students picked for live demos, tested on camera the day before
- Week 1 reveal clip and the question wall slide
- Certificates named and ready
- Feedback form link
- Written parent consent for any video used outside the class

Students bring:
- Robot charged, final code uploaded
- Parents invited to join

## Run of show (90 min)

### 0 to 10 min · Engage: Where we started

Play the week 1 reveal clip, then show the question wall. Answer three of the class's first questions using what they now know.

### 10 to 60 min · Elaborate: Showcase

Play the 5-minute montage. Then 8 to 10 live demos of 3 to 4 minutes each: the robot standing, three taps, and one thing the student learnt. Parents ask questions in chat.

### 60 to 75 min · Explore: Push challenge _(trim if short)_

Volunteer robots on camera. A bigger push each round until one robot is left standing.

### 75 to 85 min · Evaluate: Certificates

Read every name. Then show next steps: extension projects and the next cohort.

### 85 to 90 min · Admin: Feedback

Five-minute feedback form for parents and students.

## Assignment (due Saturday 8 PM ET)

### Must · T1: Final video

60 to 90 seconds, landscape, whole robot in frame. Follow the brief. Due the Saturday before demo day.

```
FINAL VIDEO (60–90 s, landscape, whole robot in frame)
0–10 s    First name, grade, and what your robot does
10–25 s   Robot standing on its own
25–40 s   Three taps, and it recovers
40–60 s   Sense, decide, act on your robot, in your words
60–80 s   One bug and how you fixed it
80–90 s   What you want to build next
```

### Must · T2: Final code and README

Final .ino plus three lines at the top: what it does, your gains, your balance point.

### Must · T3: Feedback form

Five minutes, filled in during or after demo day.

### Stretch · T4: Ultrasonic stop

Shift the target angle 1 to 2 degrees forward so the robot creeps. When something is closer than 20 cm, go back to the balance point. 30-second video.

### Stretch · T5: Bluetooth control

Read single characters from a phone Bluetooth terminal app: f creeps forward, b creeps back, s stops. Each one shifts the target angle. 30-second video.

## What good looks like

- Video follows the brief and shows a stand and three taps
- Parent consent recorded before any video is shared publicly

## 1:1 focus this week

Optional this week. Review extension attempts and suggest a next project.

## Teacher notes

- Both extensions are future scope. Without encoders, the robot moves by shifting its target angle, so it will creep and drift. Present them as experiments, not requirements.
- The Tumbller has a Bluetooth module. Check Elegoo's pin map for how it connects before you give the Bluetooth task.
