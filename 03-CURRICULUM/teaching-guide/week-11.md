# Week 11: Submission week

> Teacher-facing. Converted from `sterling-teaching-guide.html` (the HTML teaching guide) on 2026-10-15. Student version of this week lives in the site repo: `content/weeks/week-11/index.md`.

**Driving question:** Can I explain every line of what my robot is doing?

| | |
|---|---|
| Class length | 90 min, 70 min short |
| Robot by the end of the week | Submitted |
| Assignment due | Saturday, 8 PM ET |

## Goal

Every student submits a working robot (30 seconds hands-free, recovers from three taps) with commented code. The live class is a pit stop with no new content.

Students leave with:
- A complete submission package
- Code they can explain section by section
- The brief for the final video

## Before class

You prepare:
- Status list: green (meets the bar), amber (stands under 30 s), red (not standing)
- Breakout rooms set by status
- Week 12 video brief ready to post

Students bring:
- Robot charged
- Latest code and tuning log open

## Run of show (90 min)

### 0 to 10 min · Explain: Submission checklist

Walk through the four items in the package and the bar: 30 seconds hands-free and recovery from three taps. Due Saturday 8 PM ET.

### 10 to 50 min · Explore: Pit stop

Breakout rooms by status. Green: comment the code, plan the final video, start an optional extension. Amber: tuning with a mentor. Red: with you. Move between rooms.

Ask:
- What have you already ruled out?

### 50 to 70 min · Evaluate: Peer code review _(trim if short)_

Pairs swap code. Each finds one line they can't explain and asks, "Why this value?" The owner answers.

### 70 to 85 min · Elaborate: Explain it back

Three or four volunteers explain their robot's loop in 60 seconds: sense, decide, act; P, D and I; the balance point.

### 85 to 90 min · Evaluate: Final video brief

Post the week 12 brief. Final video due the Saturday before demo day.

## Assignment (due Saturday 8 PM ET)

### Must · T1: Final code

One .ino with a comment heading each section: read sensor, angle, error, P, D, I, cut-off, motors.

### Must · T2: The bar, on video

Up to 45 s, one take: 30 seconds standing, then three taps.

### Must · T3: Final values and reflection

Fill in and paste as text.

```
FINAL VALUES
Balance point: ____°   Kp: ____   Kd: ____   Ki: ____
Cut-off angle: ____°   Dead zone L / R: ____ / ____

REFLECTION
1. What was hardest?
2. Which bug took longest, and how did you find it?
3. In your own words, what does D do?
4. If you rebuilt it, what would you change?
5. What do you want to build next?
```

### Must · T4: Oral check

In your 1:1 this week, explain your code section by section in about 5 minutes.

## What good looks like

- Stands 30 seconds hands-free
- Recovers from three taps
- Every section of the code has a comment
- Explains P and D in the 1:1 without notes
- Reflection complete

## 1:1 focus this week

Five-minute oral check: the student walks you through their code. This is how you know the work is theirs.

## Teacher notes

- Decide the certificate rule before the cohort starts. Suggestion: a certificate of completion for a full submission, with a "Balanced" mark for meeting the bar.
- Students who miss the bar submit their best attempt and a bug story by the week 12 class.
